## Hi, I'm Sophia Soriano!

Pronouns: she/her

I'm originally from Santa Barbara, CA, and grew up swimming and surfing along the coast. This childhood connection to the water grew into a fascination with marine life and ocean conservation. In college, I paired that pull toward marine science with a growing fascination for problem-solving, studying Biological Sciences and Computer Science. Career opportunities pointed me toward medical research, and I spent three years as an R&D Chemist at NuSil Technologies LLC, gaining experience in analytical methods for validating drug delivery silicone formulations, before earning an MS in Biology with a focus in Bioinformatics from the University of Oregon. In parallel, as a Computational Biologist at Oregon Health & Science University (OHSU)'s Cancer Early Detection Advanced Research Center (CEDAR), I co-authored a publication on the discovery of novel prostate cancer diagnostic biomarkers and led an assessment of a new protein quantification method SOMAscan against standard mass spectrometry approaches.
Through this first chapter of my scientific career, I've remained moored to my fascination with marine environments. I'm currently part of the ![UCSB MEDS](https://bren.ucsb.edu/masters-programs/master-environmental-data-science) 2027 cohort learning to apply my analytical and computational toolkit to challenges much closer to my heart: marine conservation, pollution remediation, and sustainability analytics.
